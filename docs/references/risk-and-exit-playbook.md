# Risk And Exit Playbook

Use this reference when turning a technical candidate into a risk-defined plan.

## Stop Logic

Use setup low plus buffer, not a fixed percent stop. The stop should represent pattern failure.

Examples:
- VCP: last contraction low or handle low plus buffer.
- U&R: undercut low plus buffer.
- Pullback: reaction low or support low plus buffer.
- Breakout: pivot/base failure level plus buffer.

## Position Sizing

Position size = dollars willing to lose / per-share risk.

Portfolio risk should normally stay near 0.5% to 2% per idea unless the user explicitly chooses otherwise.

## Targets And Exits

- Prefer ideas with at least 3R to 5R potential.
- Move toward breakeven after 1R to 2R only when price confirms.
- Trail using higher lows, swing lows, or relevant moving averages depending on regime.
- Sell into strength when price becomes vertical, volume expands climactically, or the move becomes extended.
