# News Causality Playbook

Use this reference when forming opportunity hypotheses from news and market environment.

Core rule: classify the event first, then map who benefits, who loses, which ETFs confirm it, and what technical evidence must appear before it becomes actionable.

## Event Classifier

1. Rates and liquidity: Fed path, yields, QE/QT, credit spreads, lending standards, dollar liquidity.
2. Inflation and cost: CPI/PPI/PCE, oil, freight, wages, food, metals, rent, margin pressure.
3. Technology cycle: AI capex, semiconductors, cloud capex, hardware inventory, PC/smartphone cycle.
4. Supply chain and capacity: shortages, gluts, shipping, ports, factory outages, inventory cycles.
5. Geopolitics and war: sanctions, conflict, export controls, defense budgets, chokepoints.
6. Policy and regulation: subsidies, bans, antitrust, tax credits, FDA/SEC/FTC decisions, industrial policy.

## Search Checklist

- What changed: demand, supply, interest rate/liquidity, regulation, or expectations?
- Which part of the supply chain captures revenue or pricing power?
- Which groups are direct winners, direct losers, and second-order beneficiaries?
- Which ETFs or group proxies should confirm the thesis?
- Is this a durable causal shift or a one-day headline?

## Output Discipline

Every hypothesis should include affected themes, related ETFs, possible beneficiaries, possible losers, historical analogues, technical confirmation needed, and confidence. Never convert a headline directly into a trade command.
