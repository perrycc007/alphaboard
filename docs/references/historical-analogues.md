# Historical Analogues

Use these cases as curated backup evidence before promoting a hypothesis.

## Rates And Liquidity

- 2004-2006: technology could outperform during hikes when earnings growth offset discount-rate pressure.
- 2020-2021: liquidity expansion favored growth, crypto, IPOs, and speculative leaders.
- 2022: rapid rate hikes pressured long-duration growth and high-beta speculative assets.

## Inflation And Cost

- 1970s inflation: commodity producers benefited while many margin-sensitive businesses struggled.
- 2021-2022 inflation surge: energy and materials led as inflation surprised upward.
- Post-pandemic freight spike: logistics winners faded once capacity normalized.

## Technology Cycle

- 1995-2000 internet buildout: infrastructure and networking captured large early value before many applications monetized.
- 2016-2018 cloud/GPU cycle: semiconductors led demand expectations.
- 2023-2024 AI capex: semiconductors, networking, power, and cooling often outperformed app-layer names.

## Supply Chain And Capacity

- 2020-2021 port congestion: freight and logistics beneficiaries surged before normalization.
- 2021-2022 chip shortage: scarce suppliers had pricing power while automakers and hardware users were constrained.
- Solar capacity gluts: demand growth failed to protect margins once supply expanded too quickly.

## Geopolitics And Policy

- Russia-Ukraine 2022: energy, defense, and selected commodities benefited from supply and budget shifts.
- CHIPS Act: semiconductor and domestic capacity beneficiaries gained a policy tailwind.
- China education/internet regulation 2021: policy destroyed prior economics despite low valuations.
